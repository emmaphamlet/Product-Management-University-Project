# Out of Scope
  
Deliverables and other items that will be considered out of scope in this project, include:
  
*   Predictive analytics for academic outcomes  - while tracking and predicting academic outcomes is very important for long-term system growth/improvement, predictive analytics will be excluded from this current system launch. We ultimately decided that this was what was best to better narrow down the focus towards core registration functionality, which is of the highest priority in this release. Integrating advanced predictive models would require comprehensive data processing and take a significant amount of time, which would not be feasible or follow the trajectory of our current timeline.
    
*   Mobile app - we will not develop a mobile app for users in this project scope for this release. While a mobile application would likely enhance overall user experience and accessibility, our main focus lies in focusing on optimizing the current web-based application. For future phases, mobile app development may be implemented, but only once the web application has demonstrated that it is both consistent and stable.
    
*   Custom reporting features - while highly valuable to student and academic success, the ability to generate personalized reports, will not be included in this initial phase of the release. Including this feature in the initial release would not be practical as it would require numerous other additional resources in order to begin creating tailored report templates and filtering options specific to users.
