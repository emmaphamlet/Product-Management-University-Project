
Stakeholders play a big part in the decisions we make, and features we implement for our proposed solution. Some of the main identified stakeholders include:
  

| Stakeholder Group<br> | Features Requested<br> | Benefits From Our Solution<br> |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Undergraduate Students: The largest affected group by the registration system.<br> | - Fasted, no crashes<br>- Mobile-first experience<br>- Back-up schedule support<br>- Degree Audit system<br>- Automatic registering (no 6am wake-up)<br>- Course waitlist feature which displays position in the line<br>- Notification system for when classes open up<br> | - Quality-of-life changes<br>- Less cumbersome registering experience<br>- More features to make their lives easier and a reliable system<br> |
| Senior Academic Advisor: Non-student user of the registration system<br> | - Accurate prereq checking<br>- Advisor-side dashboard with all student info displayed<br>- Reporting tool to find students unable to get courses<br>- Multi-lingual support for international students & visa compliance checking<br>  <br> | - Quality of life improvements<br>- Less reliance on multiple systems if everything is unified for them in one place<br>- Directly benefits sub-group of international students<br> |
| Department Advisor: Manages course spots and TA assignments<br> | - Easy way to adjust course capacities and TA assignments<br>- Notification system for affected students<br> | - Less reliance on manual tasking, improving job efficiency<br>- Benefits TA’s & students indirectly<br> |
| Graduate Program Director: Manages grad programs and interfaces directly with system<br> | - Variable credit-hour courses<br>- Cross-listing course management<br>- Flexibility to register independent-study <br>- Prereq checks for grad courses<br>- Thesis/dissertation tracking integration<br>- Course-enrollment analytics<br> | - Will provide quality of life improvements for director<br>- Benefits grad students<br>- Unified system instead of tracking data on multiple<br> |
| Chief Information Officer: manages IT side of the system<br> | - ‘scalable and reliable’<br>- secure<br>- FERPA and ‘other relevant regulation’ compliance<br>- ‘API-First’ design<br>- 99.99% uptime or 52.56min/year downtime<br>- Logging/monitoring capabilities<br>- IT dashboard<br>- Automated testing<br>- Less downtime during updates<br>- Wants <2 second transactions <br>- Wants RTO of 4 hrs and RPO of 15 minutes<br> | - A more reliable system which needs less maintenance<br>- A clear/easy way to see and address problems when they happen<br>- Easier fixing of issues and ability to continuously roll out updates<br> |
| Disability Services Coordinator: Assists disabled students through registration <br> | - Screen readers/assistive technologies<br>- WCAG 2.1 compliance<br>- Flexible registration support for students in-need<br>– Accessibility mode<br>- Integration with accommodation management system <br> | - Mainly affects disabled students more than coordinator<br>- Unifying multiple systems into one<br> |
| Financial Aid Director: Interacts with course reg system to help students who need financial aid<br> | - Automatic aid eligibility checks<br>- Disbursement system integration<br>- Improved compliance reporting<br>- Cost reports during registration<br>- Scholarship system integration<br> | - Benefits financially challenged students<br>- Helps director by unifying systems and automating his work<br> |
| Student Success Coordinator: Finds and helps struggling students<br> | - Automatic detection of struggling students<br>- Internal tool to communicate with students<br>- Data on course grades for students to see<br>- Integrated tutoring support<br> | - Automates parts of their job<br>- Helps struggling students get necessary resources<br> |
