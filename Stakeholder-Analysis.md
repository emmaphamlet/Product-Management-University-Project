
**Stakeholders Page**
  
Stakeholders play a big part in the decisions we make, and features we implement for our proposed solution. Some of the main identified stakeholders include:
  

| Stakeholder Group<br> | Core Challenges/Frustrations<br> | Benefits From Our Solution<br> |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Undergraduate Students<br> | - System is slow and occasionally crashes<br>- Hard to use on mobile-devices<br>- Can only keep one schedule saved<br>- No Degree Audit system<br>- Manual submitting required<br>- No waitlist status on courses<br>- Can’t tell when spots in classes open up<br> | - Less cumbersome registering experience<br>- More features to make their lives easier and a reliable system<br> |
| Senior Academic Advisor<br> | - Prerequisite checking is inaccurate <br>- Advisors can’t see all student info in one spot<br>- Backup schedules aren’t supported<br>- Hard to find students struggling to get the courses they need<br>- International students have trouble understanding english-only interface and can’t easily find visa compliance information<br>  <br> | - Quality of life improvements<br>- Less reliance on multiple systems if everything is unified for them in one place<br>- Directly benefits sub-group of international students<br> |
| Department Administrator<br> | - Adjusting course capacities and TA assignments is cumbersome<br>- Cross-listed courses are difficult to manage<br>- Students have no idea when their courses and schedules are modified<br> | - Less reliance on manual tasking, improving job efficiency<br>- Benefits TA’s & students indirectly<br> |
| Graduate Program Director<br> | - Courses have only fixed credit-hours which doesn’t work for grad students<br>- Cross-listing course management is not handled well by the system<br>- Independent study is not trackable<br>- Prereq checks for grad courses are inaccurate<br>- Thesis/dissertation tracking integration is not present<br>- No Course-enrollment analytics<br> | - Will provide quality of life improvements for director<br>- Benefits grad students<br>- Unified system instead of tracking data on multiple<br> |
| Chief Information Officer<br> | - Current system breaks often and doesn’t scale<br>- System does not meet security and encryption regulations such as FERPA<br>- Integrations are clunky<br>- Current system sees too much downtime with minimal logging/monitoring<br>- No IT dashboard to monitor diagnostics<br>- Testing application is manual and updates cause too much downtime<br>- Transactions vary in completion time<br> | - A more reliable system which needs less maintenance<br>- A clear/easy way to see and address problems when they happen<br>- Easier fixing of issues and ability to continuously roll out updates<br> |
| Disability Services Coordinator<br> | - No WCAG 2.1 accessibility standards compliance<br>- No accommodations for special-needs students<br>– No simple UI accessibility mode<br>- No accommodation system integration<br> | - Mainly affects disabled students more than coordinator<br>- Unifying multiple systems into one<br> |
| Financial Aid Director<br> | - aid eligibility checks are manual<br>- aid disbursement is not integrated<br>- Financial aid compliance is unclear<br>- Registration doesn’t automatically generate cost reports<br>- Scholarships aren’t integrated into course system<br> | - Benefits financially challenged students by ensuring all compliance checking and disbursement is in one place<br>- Helps director by unifying systems and making the process simpler<br> |
| Student Success Coordinator<br> | - Struggling students need to be found manually<br>- Communication to students in-need has to occur separately<br>- Students cannot see grade distributions for courses and don’t know success probabilities<br>- Academic help is not integrated and is managed in separate systems<br> | - Automates parts of their job<br>- Helps struggling students get necessary resources<br> |
