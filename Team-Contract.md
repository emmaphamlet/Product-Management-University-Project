Project Responsibility Rotation
-------------------------------

Our team will rotate through the following roles for each project deliverable to ensure everyone gains experience in different aspects of project management and systems development:

### Core Roles for All Deliverables

### Product Manager/Owner

*   ### Represents stakeholder and user interests for this deliverable.
    
*   ### Maintains vision alignment and ensures business value.
    
*   ### Makes prioritization decisions regarding features/content.
    
*   ### Accepts/rejects final deliverable based on quality criteria.
    
*   ### Communicates with instructor/TA regarding requirements.
    

### Scrum Master/Facilitator

*   ### Facilitates team process and removes impediments.
    
*   ### Coordinates meetings and ensures productive collaboration.
    
*   ### Tracks progress and helps the team stay on schedule.
    
*   ### Ensures agile principles are followed during development.
    
*   ### Promotes continuous improvement in team processes.
    

### Technical Lead/Architect

*   ### Provides technical direction and ensures solution integrity.
    
*   ### Makes key design decisions regarding system structure.
    
*   ### Ensures technical components are properly integrated.
    
*   ### Guides technical documentation and modeling approaches.
    
*   ### Addresses complex technical challenges as they arise.
    

### Quality Assurance/User Advocate

*   ### Develops acceptance criteria based on user needs and rubric.
    
*   ### Represents the user perspective throughout development.
    
*   ### Conducts systematic reviews against requirements.
    
*   ### Tests deliverables for completeness and user experience.
    
*   ### Ensures documentation meets professional standards.
