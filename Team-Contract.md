| **STRATEGIES:** What strategies can we employ to promote systems thinking, a suspension of assumptions, viewing one another as colleagues, and developing facilitation skills by all team members? What methods of giving and receiving feedback will best support us?<br> |
| **Suspension of assumptions:** We understand that making assumptions about each other’s ideas, the problem, or the solution can lead to oversight, miscommunications, and incorrect problem-solving. To prevent assumptions from occurring, we will always promote asking questions both about the problem, and to each other. If we always make an effort to fully understand, the risk of assumptions decreases.<br>**View one another as Colleagues:** Viewing each other as colleagues is a good way to promote systems thinking because it asserts a mutual trusting of one another’s ideas, and problem-solving skills. To think of each other as integral parts of a team allows us to autonomously function on our respective work and come together to a final product without constantly being questioned, and needing to explain thought-processes before working.<br>**Developing facilitation skills by all team members:** To promote this important skill, we will ensure to rotate group roles every so often including in our weekly stand-up meetings to give everyone a chance to ‘lead the group’ and facilitate discussion. Letting each group-member take the wheel will allow us to truly grasp each other’s perspective and let us learn from each other.  <br>  <br>**Giving and Receiving Feedback:** We will treat feedback as strictly constructive and never as an attack on character. To ensure this, the feedback giver needs to play a part to keep feedback strictly constructive and about specific aspects of work rather than personal attributes. Feedback receivers should also know that feedback isn’t intended to be personal, but more so an attempt to improve themselves for the betterment of the group and everyone’s learning. For our actual process of giving feedback, it will only occur at the end of scheduled meetings for the purpose of maintaining productivity and team connection. Feedback will only be given if all group members besides the one receiving feedback agree that the feedback is needed. This ensures that only major grievances are handled with feedback rather than smaller, personal issues between group-members.<br> |

Project Responsibility Rotation
-------------------------------

Our team will rotate through the following roles for each project deliverable to ensure everyone gains experience in different aspects of project management and systems development:

### Core Roles for All Deliverables

### Product Manager/Owner

*   ### Represents stakeholder and user interests for this deliverable.
    
*   ### Maintains vision alignment and ensures business value.
    
*   ### Makes prioritization decisions regarding features/content.
    
*   ### Accepts/rejects final deliverable based on quality criteria.
    
*   ### Communicates with instructor/TA regarding requirements.
    

### Scrum Master/Facilitator

*   ### Facilitates team process and removes impediments.
    
*   ### Coordinates meetings and ensures productive collaboration.
    
*   ### Tracks progress and helps the team stay on schedule.
    
*   ### Ensures agile principles are followed during development.
    
*   ### Promotes continuous improvement in team processes.
    

### Technical Lead/Architect

*   ### Provides technical direction and ensures solution integrity.
    
*   ### Makes key design decisions regarding system structure.
    
*   ### Ensures technical components are properly integrated.
    
*   ### Guides technical documentation and modeling approaches.
    
*   ### Addresses complex technical challenges as they arise.
    

### Quality Assurance/User Advocate

*   ### Develops acceptance criteria based on user needs and rubric.
    
*   ### Represents the user perspective throughout development.
    
*   ### Conducts systematic reviews against requirements.
    
*   ### Tests deliverables for completeness and user experience.
    
*   ### Ensures documentation meets professional standards.
