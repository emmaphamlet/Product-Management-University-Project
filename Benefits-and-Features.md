
**Benefits & Features**
  
Based on our research and stakeholder analysis, we have identified the following necessary features for the new rendition of the GSU course registration system.
  
These features all address critical stakeholder needs and provide functionality which supports the goals and objectives of GSU and its stakeholders including supporting a registration system and integrations with other systems to help support the faculty which ensure academic success in the GSU student body.

**1.  Automated Waitlist Management**
    
*   Description: Allows users to receive real-time updates on classes when a seat opens up through SMS and email notifications. 
    
*   Benefit: Increases efficiency, usability, and transparency in the student course registration process. Also reduces manual monitoring of the registration process and unnecessary advisor intervention. 
    
  
  
**2.  Advisor Dashboard**
    
*   Description: Compiles together all student data including courses, degree data, financial info, academics, and personal information into one place
    
*   Benefit: Enables advisors to search a student and see the ‘big picture’ view of their status at the university. Advisors will be able to help students quicker and see more information in one place instead of having to search other GSU systems or external systems.
    
  
  
**3.  Real- Time Course Availability Tracker**
    
*   Description: A dynamic, real- time dashboard that integrated with the university course registration database to provide continuously updated information on course status. The system displays live data such as the total number of available seats, current enrollment, waitlist position, and peak registration traffic. It will also offer alerts when a spot opens or when a user’s preferred course becomes available.
    
*   Benefits: For students, it will eliminate uncertainty by providing up-to-the-minute status on courses, enables quick, informed decisions; reduces time spent refreshing or manually updating. And advisors and administrative stuff alleviates the burden of answering repetitive availability questions, freeing up time for complex advising. The GSU IT & Operation team reduces backend load from students repeatedly refreshing pages, improving system performance and stability during peak times; helps identify bottlenecks in the course scheduling process.
